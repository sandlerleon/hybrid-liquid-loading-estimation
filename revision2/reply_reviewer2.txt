Thank you for this comment, which is justified. The manuscript has been rewritten from beginning to end rather than corrected locally; its structure, prose and typesetting are new, and no calculation, result or conclusion has changed. The full response, with a table of correspondence between the two versions, is in the uploaded file FMI_Response_to_Reviewers_R2.docx. In brief:

1. Equations. All seventeen equations are now display equations typeset in the Office Math equation editor, with proper fractions, radicals, subscripts and superscripts and the equation number at the right margin. In the previous version they were plain text strings such as “Q_ind = Q_G · φ(X_LM, ρ_G/ρ_L, Fr_G)”, which is what the reviewer saw. Symbols in the running text are set the same way: italic variables and upright labels, so that X_LM, u₁ and ρ₁₂ no longer appear with underscores. The equations are gathered in one section (Section 3, Mathematical model), which opens by stating the order in which they are applied. Each group is introduced by a sentence and followed by a paragraph that explains its origin and assumptions.

2. Program-code content removed. Algorithm 1, which was written as pseudo-code, is removed. The decision procedure is now described in prose (Section 2.4), and the order of the calculation is given in the opening paragraph of Section 3. Script and function names no longer appear in the main text; they are confined to the Data availability statement and to the provenance table in Appendix A (Table A1).

3. Development-log content removed. Statements about what had been added or left unchanged in the revision are removed. Examples are “(new)”, “the new analyses are in Sections 5.9–5.12” and “Tables 3, 5 and 6 and Figs 2–8 are the original results and are unchanged”. The two Monte Carlo samples remain, because they are a property of the method; they are now described once, in Section 3.2, as part of the design of the study.

4. Structure. The paper now runs in a single sequence: research problem, estimation architecture, mathematical model, computational design, results, discussion and conclusions. The four-part introduction with its numbered list of contributions is now one continuous introduction. It moves from the literature to the gap and then to three research questions, whose answers are stated at the end of the introduction and again in the conclusions. Third-level headings, bold run-in labels and bulleted lists, including the bulleted Limitations, are replaced by connected paragraphs. Each Results subsection opens with the question it answers and closes with its interpretation. The validation design, previously in the Methods, is now in the Discussion (Section 5.5), next to the limitations it addresses.

5. Provenance and verification. A new Section 3.1 states the origin of each equation (established, adapted from the literature, or specific to this study) and its assumptions. Appendix A gives this equation by equation (Table A1) and reports the executable checks of the implementation (Table A2). The implementation details that the reviewer found intrusive in the text are kept, but in this one place.

6. Figures, references and nomenclature. The figure labels now match the text. The internal acronym “SWCL” and the shorthand “E1/E2” are removed from Figs. 2, 3 and 12. The title of Fig. 7 no longer uses “accuracy”, which the paper reserves for measured quantities. Fig. 1 now shows the consistency index with the covariance term of Eq. (11) and uses the symbols of the text. The figures were regenerated from the deposited code with the fixed seeds, and their data are unchanged. References are numbered in order of citation. The nomenclature, with a units column, is placed after the Conclusions.

Equation, table and figure numbers are unchanged. Because the text was rewritten throughout, a change-marked copy would mark every paragraph; the correspondence table in the response file is provided in its place.
